Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Life OS is a personal tool used by one person, its owner, on their own Google account. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages and settings to build a daily action list, and applies labels and filters the owner has approved.
  • Google Drive: creates and updates only the files the app itself makes (a daily summary document).

How data is handled

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and stored there.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not send email, delete email, or change sharing on Drive files.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Life OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
